A museum placard for an ordinary office object treated as an ancient artifact: a stapler, recast as a ritual press that wed two sheets with a single tooth — catalogued with its date (c. 1998), its material (steel and spring), and its donor (the filing clerks, who believed no page should die alone).

## How it works

`main.py` builds the placard from three fragments, keeps two rejected drafts beside it with the reason each one lost, and checks the winner before writing it: 1–180 characters, at most 35 words, UTF-8, not only whitespace, one line, no trailing newline. The checked line goes to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
